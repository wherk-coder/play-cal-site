## 4. Children's Privacy and COPPA Compliance

Play Cal is designed for use by parents and guardians. **Children under 13 are not permitted to create accounts or use the Service independently.**

### 4.1 Parental Consent

When you add children's information to the Service (such as names and ages for scheduling), you represent that:

- You are the parent or legal guardian of the child
- You have authority to provide this information
- You consent to our collection and use of this information as described in this Privacy Policy

### 4.2 Children's Information We Collect

With parental consent, we may collect:

- Child's first name and age
- Scheduling information related to the child
- Event participation data

We do NOT collect:

- Children's contact information (email, phone, address)
- Children's photos or biometric data
- Location tracking of children
- Children's social media profiles or identifiers

### 4.3 How We Use Children's Information

Children's information is used solely for:

- Creating and managing family schedules
- Coordinating playdates and events
- Displaying event participants to authorized family members

### 4.4 Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:

- Review the children's information we have collected
- Request deletion of their children's information
- Refuse to allow further collection or use of their children's information
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@play-cal.com. You can also delete your children's information yourself at any time by deleting your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account).

### 4.5 Data Retention for Children

We retain children's information only as long as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law. Upon request, we will delete children's information within 30 days.
